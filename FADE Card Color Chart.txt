[Null]
Primary: Item cards, Void.
Secondary: Copying cards or abilities.
Tertiary: Basic catch-all for other Elements or abilities, usually converting them to Null-type.

[Fire]
Primary: High Damage and destruction.
Secondary: Powerlevel Boosting, Aura and Drain Counters.
Tertiary: Various other Ailments.

[Water]
Primary: Card advantage.
Secondary: Stax or Punish, usually with Taxes.
Tertiary: Protection effects, Paralyze Counters.

[Earth]
Primary: High HP, Fracture Counters, destruction effects.
Secondary: Anti-Augment, Anti-Equip, semi-high Damage.
Tertiary: Character cost reduction, Damage reduction or prevention.

[Storm]
Primary: Power Counters, Flash, Multistrike.
Secondary: Other Resource generation and counterplay.
Tertiary: Paralyze Counters.

[Nature]
Primary: Poison Counters.
Secondary: Mill, Discard, Returning cards from your Discard Pile.
Tertiary: Varied Support, Powerlevel Boosting.

[Metal]
Primary: Lock-On, Arm Up, Equip.
Secondary: Damage alterations, Shield Counters.
Tertiary: Very high Damage, Max HP reduction, Damage Counters.

[Light]
Primary: Healing, Barrier Counters, Protection effects.
Secondary: Low Damage, Anthem Buffs.
Tertiary: Varied Support, Stax, Reveal effects.

[Darkness]
Primary: Ailments, Concussion, Deathmark, Knock Outs.
Secondary: High Damage, Curse.
Tertiary: Varied Support, Bullying effects.

[Time]
Primary: Low Damage, Charge, Very High TP generation.
Secondary: Game State manipulation
Tertiary: Replay, Rapid Plays, Ailment and Damage Self for power.

[Space]
Primary: Imprison, Imprison Self for power.
Secondary: Effect Recursion, Max TP alteration.
Tertiary: Varied Support, Damage alterations.
